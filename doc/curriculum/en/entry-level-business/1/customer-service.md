# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of an Entry Level Certificate / Level 1 qualification in
Business and Customer Service (DfE, ages 14-16). This is the
alternative vocational route for learners who aren't sitting full
GCSEs: every unit is a real, work-readiness skill, taught through
scenarios the learner can act out. The cards stay concrete — every
abstract idea (feedback, non-verbal, complaint) is anchored in a
real example (a customer at a till, a phone ringing, a complaint
form) before the word is named.
