# Índice
- What is a business — A business provides something people want
- What is a business — Different types of business: sole trader, partnership, company
- What is a business — Local, national and online businesses
- Business sectors — Goods: things you can touch (clothes, food, books)
- Business sectors — Services: things someone does for you (haircut, repair, lesson)
- Working in business — Common jobs: shop assistant, receptionist, warehouse worker
- Working in business — The working day: start, break, finish
- Working in business — Pay, payslip, tax basics, in simple terms
- Working in business — Rights at work: safety, fair pay, fair treatment
- Money in business — Money in and money out: the basics of cash flow
- Money in business — Saving money: why businesses keep some money aside
- Money in business — Spending money: wages, rent, supplies
- Teamwork — Working with other people: clear communication, helping out
- Teamwork — Following instructions from a manager
- Teamwork — Sorting out a small disagreement at work

# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of an Entry Level Certificate / Level 1 qualification in
Business and Administration (DfE, ages 14-16). The unit gives the
learner a very simple, real-world picture of what a business is,
what jobs there are, and what it's like to work in one — useful
context for the customer-service, IT and admin units that follow.
Vocabulary stays concrete: every abstract idea (cash flow,
sectors, partnership) is anchored in a real-life example the
learner has met (a shop on the high street, a payslip they have
seen, a disagreement in the classroom).
