# Índice
- Working in a team — Communicating clearly with colleagues
- Working in a team — Passing information to the right person
- Working in a team — Helping out when it's busy
- Customer service in person — Welcoming a customer: the first 30 seconds
- Customer service in person — Reading the customer's mood and matching it
- Customer service in person — Handling a busy queue without losing patience
- Customer service on the phone — Answering, putting on hold, transferring a call
- Customer service on the phone — Speaking to a customer who is upset
- Customer service on the phone — Ending a call politely and making a clear note
- Customer service in writing — Replying to a customer email or message
- Customer service in writing — Using a polite, professional tone in writing
- Customer service in writing — Spelling, grammar and tone: the basics in writing
- Problem-solving — Listening carefully to find the real problem
- Problem-solving — Offering a clear fix the customer can accept
- Problem-solving — Knowing when to ask a colleague or manager for help
- Continuous improvement — Saying what went well after helping a customer
- Continuous improvement — Saying what to do differently next time

# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of an Entry Level Certificate / Level 1 qualification in
Business and Customer Service (DfE, ages 15-16). The Year 2 unit
extends Year 1's customer-service foundation by adding the
teamwork and problem-solving strands, and by looking at customer
service in three real modes (in person, on the phone, in writing).
Cards stay short and anchored in concrete scenarios from real
workplaces (a busy shop till, a phone ringing, a polite reply
email).
