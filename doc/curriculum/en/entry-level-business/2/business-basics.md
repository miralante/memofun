# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of an Entry Level Certificate / Level 1 qualification in
Business and Administration (DfE, ages 15-16). The Year 2 unit
extends Year 1's business-basics picture with the office and
retail-specific jobs, the basics of office technology, and the
simple health-and-safety and equality-and-inclusion strands that
every workplace needs. Vocabulary stays anchored in a real-life
example (a real till, a real office printer, a real safety notice)
before the word is named.
