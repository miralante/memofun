# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of Primary school in England (6-7 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
Geography at Key Stage 1) published by the Department for Education.
Year 2 extends Year 1 by comparing a small area of the UK with a
small area in a contrasting non-European country, introduces the
basic physical vocabulary (coast, river, mountain, hill, valley,
equator, North and South Poles) and the basic map skills (key,
title, compass directions, aerial photographs). Schools pick the
contrasting country themselves — common choices include a village
in Kenya or India.
