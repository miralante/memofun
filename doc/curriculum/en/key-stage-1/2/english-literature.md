# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of Primary school in England (6-7 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
English Literature at Key Stage 1) published by the Department for
Education. Year 2 widens the Year 1 diet of traditional tales with
classic European fairy tales, the first fables, and the first
"chapter book" reads. Memofun covers the **literature** strand only
(stories, rhymes, poems, authors); phonics, spelling, grammar,
punctuation, and handwriting are intentionally **out of scope** for
this subject — see the README in this folder for the rationale.
