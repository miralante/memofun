# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of Primary school in England (6-7 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
History at Key Stage 1) published by the Department for Education.
Year 2 extends Year 1's "lives of significant individuals" and
"events beyond living memory that are significant nationally or
globally" strands. Schools pick which individuals and events to
focus on — the cards here use commonly taught examples (the Great
Fire of London, the Mayflower, early railways and flight) that
schools can swap for local equivalents if needed.
