# Índice
- Place knowledge — Where I live: my home, my street, my town or city
- Place knowledge — The four countries of the United Kingdom: England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland
- Place knowledge — The capital city of each UK country
- Place knowledge — The seas around the UK: the North Sea, the Irish Sea, the Atlantic Ocean, the English Channel
- Human and physical geography — What "human" features are: houses, roads, shops, schools
- Human and physical geography — What "physical" features are: hills, rivers, the sea, the weather
- Human and physical geography — The weather: hot, cold, rainy, sunny, windy
- Human and physical geography — The four seasons and how the weather changes
- Geographical skills — Looking at a simple map of my classroom or my school
- Geographical skills — Drawing a simple map: a road, a house, a park
- Geographical skills — Using words like near, far, next to, behind, in front of
- Fieldwork — Going outside to look at the place where I live

# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of Primary school in England (5-6 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
Geography at Key Stage 1) published by the Department for Education:
KS1 Geography covers "place knowledge" (small areas of the UK),
"human and physical geography" (basic vocabulary and the four
seasons), and "geographical skills" (simple maps, directions,
fieldwork). Year 1 starts with the learner's own home and local
area — the rest of the UK comes second.
