# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of Primary school in England (5-6 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
History at Key Stage 1) published by the Department for Education:
KS1 History covers "changes within living memory", "events beyond
living memory that are significant nationally or globally", and
"the lives of significant individuals in the past who have
contributed to national and international achievements". Year 1
focuses on the learner's own life and family, plus one or two very
concrete events / people from the national story. Abstract
periods and dates stay out — at this age the learner is meeting
"time" as something they can point to in their own house.
