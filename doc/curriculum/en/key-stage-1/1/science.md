# Índice
- Working scientifically — Asking simple questions about the world
- Working scientifically — Observing things closely with my senses
- Working scientifically — Sorting and grouping objects by what they share
- Working scientifically — Saying what I notice and what I wonder
- Plants — Naming common plants I see outside
- Plants — The parts of a plant: root, stem, leaf, flower
- Plants — What a plant needs to grow: light, water, warmth
- Plants — Trees: deciduous and evergreen
- Animals — Naming common animals: pets, farm, wild
- Animals — Animals with backbones (fish, birds, mammals, reptiles, amphibians)
- Animals — What animals eat: herbivores, carnivores, omnivores
- Animals — How animals move: walk, swim, fly, slither, hop

- Everyday materials — Saying what a material is like: hard, soft, rough, smooth, shiny, dull

- Seasonal changes — What changes when winter comes
- Seasonal changes — What changes when spring comes
- Seasonal changes — What changes when summer comes

# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of Primary school in England (5-6 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
Science at Key Stage 1) published by the Department for Education:
Year 1 covers "Plants", "Animals, including humans", "Everyday
materials" and "Seasonal changes", plus the "Working scientifically"
strands that run through every topic. Vocabulary is concrete and
drawn from the classroom, the playground, and the home — no
abstract concepts before they have been met as something the learner
can point at.
