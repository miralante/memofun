# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of Primary school in England (5-6 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
English Literature at Key Stage 1) published by the Department for
Education. Year 1 focuses on listening to and joining in with
traditional tales, nursery rhymes, and simple picture books —
the books most schools actually read aloud at this age. Memofun
covers the **literature** strand only (the stories, rhymes, poems,
and authors); phonics, spelling, grammar, punctuation, and
handwriting are intentionally **out of scope** for this subject —
the audience meets English as a second or additional language, so
literature (the bit that travels well across cultures) makes a
better review deck than orthography does.
