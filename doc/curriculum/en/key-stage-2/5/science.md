# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of Upper Key Stage 2 in England (9-10 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
Science at Key Stage 2) published by the Department for Education.
Year 5 brings three big new KS2 topics — "Living things and their
habitats", "Earth and space" and "Properties and changes of
materials" — alongside the continuing "Animals, including humans"
strand and the first "Forces" topic. The vocabulary gets noticeably
more technical (gravitational force, reversible change, orbit);
the cards anchor every new word in a concrete everyday image
before naming it.
