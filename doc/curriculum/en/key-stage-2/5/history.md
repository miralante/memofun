# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of Upper Key Stage 2 in England (9-10 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
History at Key Stage 2) published by the Department for Education.
Year 5 brings the first KS2 depth study of an ancient civilisation
(commonly the Ancient Greeks) plus a second non-European depth study
(commonly the Maya). Schools may pick alternatives for either —
the cards here use the most common choice but flag where a school
might swap.
