# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of Upper Key Stage 2 in England (9-10 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
English Literature at Key Stage 2) published by the Department for
Education. Year 5 brings the first real 19th-century classics
(Dickens, Stevenson, Twain, Brontë, Nesbit) alongside a strong
selection of modern children's classics (Sachar, Boyne, Almond,
Morpurgo), the first introduction to drama (a play read in class, a
retold Shakespeare story), and a proper poetry timeline that goes
from the Romantics to the present day. Memofun covers the
**literature** strand only — stories, poems, drama, and authors;
phonics, spelling, grammar, punctuation, and handwriting are
intentionally **out of scope** for this subject.
