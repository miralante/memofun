# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of Lower Key Stage 2 in England (8-9 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
English Literature at Key Stage 2) published by the Department for
Education. Year 4 widens Year 3 with the first proper "classic novel"
selection (Carroll, Grahame, Barrie, Burnett, White, Lewis) and the
first formal mythology blocks (Greek, Norse, plus a slot for a
non-European mythology chosen by the school). The named authors
move into modern children's classics (Lewis, Pullman, Wilson,
Walliams). Memofun covers the **literature** strand only — stories,
myths, poems, and authors; phonics, spelling, grammar, punctuation,
and handwriting are intentionally **out of scope** for this subject.
