# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of Lower Key Stage 2 in England (8-9 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
Geography at Key Stage 2) published by the Department for Education.
Year 4 extends Year 3's "compare a region of the UK with a region in
another country" strand by focusing the physical-geography lens on
rivers and the human-geography lens on settlement, land use and
economic activity. Six-figure grid references arrive in Year 4 —
cards stay focused on the rule (eastings first, then northings) and
on reading one off an OS map.
