# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of Lower Key Stage 2 in England (8-9 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
History at Key Stage 2) published by the Department for Education.
Year 4 continues the KS2 chronological sequence with three full
period studies: Roman Britain, Anglo-Saxon Britain, and the Viking
age, plus a local depth study chosen by the school. The Roman /
Anglo-Saxon / Viking sequence is dense in named dates and people;
cards stay small and concrete (an artefact, a place-name, a
longship) so the chronology is anchored in pictures rather than in
abstract period labels.
