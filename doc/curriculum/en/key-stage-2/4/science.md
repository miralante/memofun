# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of Lower Key Stage 2 in England (8-9 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
Science at Key Stage 2) published by the Department for Education.
Year 4 introduces three new KS2 topics — "Living things and their
habitats", "States of matter" and "Sound" — alongside the
continuing "Animals, including humans" strand and the first
"Electricity" topic. Vocabulary continues to grow (digestive
system, water cycle, amplitude, circuit), always anchored in
something concrete first.
