# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of Lower Key Stage 2 in England (7-8 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
English Literature at Key Stage 2) published by the Department for
Education. Year 3 starts the formal KS2 literature diet: classic
fairy tales in their collected forms (Grimm, Perrault), the first
English myths and legends (Arthur, Robin Hood, Saint George), the
first named "real" authors (Morpurgo, King-Smith, Dahl), and a
genuinely broader poetry selection (rhyme, rhythm, nonsense,
images). Memofun covers the **literature** strand only — stories,
rhymes, poems, and authors; phonics, spelling, grammar, punctuation,
and handwriting are intentionally **out of scope** for this subject.
