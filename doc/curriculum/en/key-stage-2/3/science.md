# Índice
- Working scientifically — Asking a clear question I can investigate
- Working scientifically — Planning a fair test: what I will change, keep the same, measure
- Working scientifically — Using simple tables and bar charts to record results
- Working scientifically — Saying what my results show in a short sentence
- Plants — The parts of a flowering plant and what each part does
- Plants — What plants need for life and how different plants survive in different places
- Plants — How water moves through a plant, from root to leaf
- Plants — How seeds are scattered: by wind, by animals, by bursting
- Animals — The importance of a balanced diet and a healthy lifestyle
- Animals — The skeleton: bones, muscles and how we move
- Animals — What the digestive system does, in simple terms
- Rocks — Naming common rocks: granite, sandstone, limestone, slate
- Rocks — Saying how a rock was made: igneous, sedimentary, metamorphic
- Rocks — How fossils form in sedimentary rock
- Rocks — What soil is made of and what it does for plants
- Light — How shadows form when light is blocked
- Light — How the Sun seems to move across the sky during the day
- Light — Sun safety: how to protect eyes and skin
- Forces and magnets — What pushes and pulls are: pushes, pulls, twists
- Forces and magnets — Magnets attract some materials and not others
- Forces and magnets — Magnets have two poles: north and south
- Forces and magnets — How magnets can attract or repel each other

# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of Lower Key Stage 2 in England (7-8 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
Science at Key Stage 2) published by the Department for Education.
Year 3 introduces three new KS2 topics — "Rocks", "Light" and
"Forces and magnets" — alongside the continuing "Plants" and
"Animals, including humans" strands. Vocabulary gets a little more
technical (igneous, sedimentary, digestive system), but every
abstract idea still comes with the concrete everyday image the
learner has already met (a real rock on the table, a real shadow on
the wall, a real magnet and paperclip).
