# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of Lower Key Stage 2 in England (7-8 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
Geography at Key Stage 2) published by the Department for Education.
Year 3 extends the KS1 "compare a small area of the UK with a small
area in a contrasting country" strand by moving from one village to a
whole region, and introduces the first proper physical-geography
vocabulary (climate zones, the water cycle, rivers, mountains,
volcanoes, earthquakes). Each new idea is anchored in a concrete
image (a real river, a real flood, a real map with contour lines)
before the word is named.
