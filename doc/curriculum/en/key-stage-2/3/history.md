# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of Lower Key Stage 2 in England (7-8 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
History at Key Stage 2) published by the Department for Education.
Year 3 starts the KS2 British-history-in-chronological-order
sequence: Stone Age → Bronze Age → Iron Age, plus an in-depth
non-European study (commonly Ancient Egypt, though schools may pick
another). Each period is met through concrete artefacts and places
(Stonehenge, Skara Brae, a mummy case) before any abstract period
vocabulary.
