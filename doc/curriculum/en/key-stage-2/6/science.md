# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of Upper Key Stage 2 in England (10-11 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
Science at Key Stage 2) published by the Department for Education.
Year 6 brings the new KS2 "Evolution and inheritance" topic — the
first real introduction to adaptation, variation and natural
selection in the curriculum — plus the second "Light" and second
"Electricity" topics. Vocabulary and concepts are now genuinely
abstract (retina, variation, natural selection, voltage); each one
still has to land in a concrete image before the word is named.
