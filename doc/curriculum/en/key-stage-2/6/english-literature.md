# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of Upper Key Stage 2 in England (10-11 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
English Literature at Key Stage 2) published by the Department for
Education. Year 6 closes KS2 with a deliberately demanding reading
diet — abridged versions of real adult classics (Dickens, Austen,
Shelley, Conan Doyle, Wells), a strong modern classics block (Collins,
Palacio, Zephaniah, Morpurgo), the first proper Shakespeare play
read in class, and a poetry block that runs from the Victorians
through war poetry to a contemporary poet chosen by the school.
Memofun covers the **literature** strand only — stories, drama,
poems, and authors; phonics, spelling, grammar, punctuation, and
handwriting are intentionally **out of scope** for this subject.
