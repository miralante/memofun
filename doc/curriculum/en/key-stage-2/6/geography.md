# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of Upper Key Stage 2 in England (10-11 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
Geography at Key Stage 2) published by the Department for Education.
Year 6 completes the KS2 "region of the UK vs region of another
continent" strand by bringing in human geography (people, work,
trade, migration) and the bigger picture of how places connect. The
first introduction to climate change and global responsibility also
arrives here — anchored in concrete examples (real floods, real
droughts, real changes on the local map) before the abstract
vocabulary.
