# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of Upper Key Stage 2 in England (10-11 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
History at Key Stage 2) published by the Department for Education.
Year 6 continues the "non-European society in depth" strand (commonly
the Benin Kingdom in West Africa) and adds the second compulsory
KS2 British depth study beyond 1066 (commonly Victorian Britain, the
First or Second World War, or another turning point chosen by the
school). Source work becomes more deliberate: students start to ask
not only what a source tells us but what it leaves out.
