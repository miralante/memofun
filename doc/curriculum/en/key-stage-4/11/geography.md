# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of GCSE in England (15-16 years old). Temario según el
GCSE Geography specification published by AQA, OCR, or Edexcel —
**pick the exam board your school uses** and review against its
current specification. Year 11 is the consolidation and exam-prep
year: a heavy focus on the issue evaluation / pre-release booklet
component and on fieldwork skills. For specific pre-release
material, check the live specification from your exam board.
