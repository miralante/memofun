# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of GCSE in England (15-16 years old). Temario según el
GCSE Physics specification published by AQA, OCR, or Edexcel —
**pick the exam board your school uses** and review against its
current specification. The Physics single-science course covers the
Physics content of Combined Science in greater depth and includes
several additional topics (momentum as a vector, the full
electromagnetic induction treatment, space and astrophysics at
Triple-only depth). AQA's "Space" topic is Triple-only; other
boards vary.
