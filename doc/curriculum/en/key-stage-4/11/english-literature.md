# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of GCSE in England (15-16 years old). Temario según el
GCSE English Literature specification published by AQA, OCR, or
Edexcel — **pick the exam board your school uses** and review
against its current set texts. Year 11 is the consolidation and
exam-prep year: closer work on the school-specific set texts and
on the unseen component. The cards here assume the same set text
shape as Year 10; review the school's actual set texts before
publishing.
