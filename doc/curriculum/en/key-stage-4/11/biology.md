# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of GCSE in England (15-16 years old). Temario según el
GCSE Biology specification published by AQA, OCR, or Edexcel —
**pick the exam board your school uses** and review against its
current specification. The Biology single-science course covers the
Biology content of Combined Science in greater depth and includes
several additional topics (monoclonal antibodies, plant disease,
the brain and eye, evolution in detail). Schools may offer Biology
across both Year 10 and Year 11; the cards here assume a
Year-10-then-Year-11 split with Year 11 covering all the topics at
the full single-science depth.
