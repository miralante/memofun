# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of GCSE in England (15-16 years old). Temario según el
GCSE Chemistry specification published by AQA, OCR, or Edexcel —
**pick the exam board your school uses** and review against its
current specification. The Chemistry single-science course covers
the Chemistry content of Combined Science in greater depth and
includes several additional topics (atom economy, percentage yield,
titration calculations, instrumental analysis, the Haber and
Contact processes).
