# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of GCSE in England (15-16 years old). Temario según el
GCSE History specification published by AQA, OCR, or Edexcel —
**pick the exam board your school uses** and review against its
current specification. Year 11 is the consolidation and exam-prep
year, often with one more depth study (a thematic study like Crime
and Punishment through time, or a chosen period) and the heavy
focus on source analysis and exam technique.
