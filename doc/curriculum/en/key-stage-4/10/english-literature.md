# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of GCSE in England (14-15 years old). Temario según el
GCSE English Literature specification published by AQA, OCR, or
Edexcel — **pick the exam board your school uses** and review
against its current set texts. Most boards share the same shape
(Shakespeare, a 19th-century novel, a modern text, a poetry
anthology); the specific texts vary. The cards here use the most
common choices (Macbeth, Jekyll and Hyde, An Inspector Calls, the
AQA Power and Conflict anthology); swap for your school's set
texts where they differ.
