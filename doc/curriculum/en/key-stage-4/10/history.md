# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of GCSE in England (14-15 years old). Temario según el
GCSE History specification published by AQA, OCR, or Edexcel —
**pick the exam board your school uses** and review against its
current specification. Most boards share a similar shape
(Cold War / superpower relations, an Elizabethan / early modern
period, a 20th-century period study); the specific periods vary
by board. The cards here use the most common choices (Cold War,
Early Elizabethan England, Weimar and Nazi Germany); swap for
your school's options where they differ. **Card design caveat**:
periods with many names and dates need many, smaller cards —
deliberate repetition for consolidation, not padding.
