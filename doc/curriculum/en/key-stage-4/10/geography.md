# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of GCSE in England (14-15 years old). Temario según el
GCSE Geography specification published by AQA, OCR, or Edexcel —
**pick the exam board your school uses** and review against its
current specification. Most boards share a similar shape (natural
hazards, ecosystems, physical landscapes, urban issues, economic
world, resource management); the specific case studies vary by
board and school. The cards here use the AQA case-study structure
as a common choice; swap for your school's choices where they
differ.
