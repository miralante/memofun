# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of GCSE in England (14-15 years old). Temario según el
GCSE Combined Science (Trilogy / Synergy / Double Award) specification
published by AQA, OCR, or Edexcel — **pick the exam board your
school uses** and review against its current specification. Combined
Science covers the three sciences at GCSE level across two GCSEs,
while still keeping the sciences in the same course as separate
content areas. Schools often run Combined Science in Year 10 and
let learners choose to specialise into single sciences in Year 11.
