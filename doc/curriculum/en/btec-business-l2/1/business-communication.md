# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of the BTEC Level 2 Diploma in Business Administration
(Pearson Edexcel). Temario según el current Pearson BTEC Level 2
Diploma specification — **review against the live specification**
before publishing, as unit numbers and titles can change between
accreditation cycles. The cards here use the units commonly
delivered in Year 1 of a two-year programme: business
communication, customer service, working in a business team,
personal selling, and branding. Every abstract idea (channel,
barriers, loyalty scheme) anchors in a real example the learner
has met before the word is named.
