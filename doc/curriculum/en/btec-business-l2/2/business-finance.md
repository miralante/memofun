# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of the BTEC Level 2 Diploma in Business Administration
(Pearson Edexcel). Temario según el current Pearson BTEC Level 2
Diploma specification — **review against the live specification**
before publishing, as unit numbers and titles can change between
accreditation cycles. The cards here use the units commonly
delivered alongside Year 2's marketing and operations block: a
deeper look at finance, a small enterprise project, and the
everyday financial documents used in a real office (invoice,
receipt, statement, petty cash). Vocabulary keeps growing
(reconciliation, break-even, invoice); every abstract idea still
anchors in a concrete example before the word is named.
