# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of secondary school in England (11-12 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
History at Key Stage 3) published by the Department for Education.
The KS3 History curriculum expects schools to teach at least three
British depth studies and at least one world depth study, covering
roughly the period from the Norman Conquest (1066) to the present.
Year 7 typically opens with the Norman Conquest and medieval
England — a long, dense period that benefits from many small,
concrete cards rather than a few sweeping ones.
