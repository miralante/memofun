# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of secondary school in England (11-12 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
Geography at Key Stage 3) published by the Department for Education.
Year 7 opens KS3 with the tectonic / weather / climate block, plus
the first real GIS (Geographic Information Systems) exposure at
school. Vocabulary continues to grow — plate tectonics, tropical
storm, climate graph — but every abstract idea still anchors in
something concrete (a real map, a real graph, a real case study).
