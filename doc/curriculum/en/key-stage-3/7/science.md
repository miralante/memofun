# Contexto o notas adicionales
First year of secondary school in England (11-12 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
Science at Key Stage 3) published by the Department for Education.
KS3 Science covers Biology, Chemistry and Physics as one subject
called "Science" (separate subject study starts at GCSE). Year 7
introduces the first proper "cells" and "particles" models — the
first time the learner meets abstract scientific models that they
can't see with the naked eye. Each new idea is anchored in a
concrete image or measurement (a real microscope, a real graph,
a real reading in newtons) before the abstract vocabulary.
