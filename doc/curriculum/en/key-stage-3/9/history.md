# Contexto o notas adicionales
Third year of secondary school in England (13-14 years old).
Temario según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study
for History at Key Stage 3) published by the Department for
Education. Year 9 typically closes KS3 with 20th-century conflict
(both World Wars and the Cold War) and a world depth study chosen
by the school. The vocabulary and concept density is now much
greater; cards stay small and concrete (a real trench, a real
ration book, a real Cold War crisis) to keep the big picture
readable.
