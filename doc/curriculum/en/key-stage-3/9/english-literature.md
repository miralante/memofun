- Drama — Shakespeare's tragedies: the structure of a fall
- Drama — Shakespeare's comedies: how they end

- Novel — A 19th-century novel end to end: Great Expectations (Dickens), Jane Eyre (Brontë), or Tess of the d'Urbervilles (Hardy)

# Contexto o notas adicionales
Third year of secondary school in England (13-14 years old). Temario
según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study for
English Literature at Key Stage 3) published by the Department for
Education. Year 9 closes KS3 with a third full Shakespeare play,
proper 19th-century and modern novels read end to end, dystopian
fiction (Orwell, Huxley), the first proper GCSE-style anthology
poetry cluster, and the first "reading like a critic" habits that
carry through into KS4. Memofun covers the **literature** strand
only — drama, novels, poems, and authors; phonics, spelling,
grammar, punctuation, and handwriting are intentionally **out of
scope** for this subject.
