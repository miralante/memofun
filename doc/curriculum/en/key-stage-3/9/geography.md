# Contexto o notas adicionales
Third year of secondary school in England (13-14 years old).
Temario según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study
for Geography at Key Stage 3) published by the Department for
Education. Year 9 typically closes KS3 with the global-economy
block: globalisation, transnational corporations, supply chains,
energy futures. The expectation is that the learner can now weigh
data sources critically and design an independent geographical
enquiry — skills that bridge into GCSE Geography at KS4.
