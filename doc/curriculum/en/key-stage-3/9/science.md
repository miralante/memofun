# Contexto o notas adicionales
Third year of secondary school in England (13-14 years old).
Temario según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study
for Science at Key Stage 3) published by the Department for
Education. Year 9 closes KS3 with the formal "inheritance and
evolution" block and the formal "forces and energy" block, ready
for the GCSE single sciences that start in KS4. The vocabulary is
now genuinely specialist (Punnett square, momentum, convection);
the cards anchor each one in a concrete image and a worked
example.
