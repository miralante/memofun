# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of secondary school in England (12-13 years old).
Temario según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study
for Science at Key Stage 3) published by the Department for
Education. Year 8 brings the first formal "atoms and elements"
strand, the first proper "waves" topic (including the
electromagnetic spectrum), and the first proper "ecology" topic
(food webs, human impact). The vocabulary is now genuinely
specialist (enzyme, pH, electromagnetic spectrum, decomposition);
the cards anchor each one in a concrete image first.
