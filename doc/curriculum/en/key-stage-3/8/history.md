# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of secondary school in England (12-13 years old).
Temario según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study
for History at Key Stage 3) published by the Department for
Education. Year 8 continues the KS3 chronological sweep through
British history with the Tudors, the Stuarts, the Industrial
Revolution, and the rise and fall of the British Empire. Schools
vary the order — the cards here assume a typical Tudors →
Stuarts → Industrial Revolution → Empire sequence.
