# Contexto o notas adicionales
Second year of secondary school in England (12-13 years old).
Temario según el English National Curriculum (programmes of study
for Geography at Key Stage 3) published by the Department for
Education. Year 8 moves the lens from the physical block of Year 7
to the human block: population, migration, urbanisation, the
development gap. Population pyramids and choropleth maps arrive
in Year 8 — both skills for reading a country at a glance from
its data.
